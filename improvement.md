# Additional Improvement

## Improvement Chosen

A dynamic "Fresh from the Oven Today" availability board was added near the top of the page.

This feature automatically displays products scheduled for the current day using JavaScript.

---

## Why This Improvement Was Added

During the diagnosis stage, one of the biggest problems identified was that customers could not quickly tell which products were available on a particular day.

Customers had to read through the full weekly schedule and work out availability themselves.

Marcus specifically mentioned that many customers visit the website while walking or commuting and need information quickly.

---

## How It Works

JavaScript checks the current day and compares it against the bakery's schedule.

Products scheduled for that day are displayed inside the availability board.

The current day is also shown so that customers can immediately understand the information being presented.

---

## Value for Customers

This improvement:

- Reduces the amount of information customers need to read.
- Helps customers quickly decide whether to visit the bakery.
- Makes important product information more visible.
- Improves the experience for mobile users.

---

## Value for the Business

This improvement supports Marcus's business goals by:

- Making product availability easier to understand.
- Reducing customer frustration.
- Encouraging more walk-in visits.
- Increasing the visibility of products available that day.

Because customers can immediately see what is available, they are more likely to stop at the bakery instead of continuing to another business.

---

## Reflection

This improvement directly addresses the most important problem identified in the client brief. Although it uses scheduled availability rather than real-time stock information, it still provides a significant improvement over the original design and adds practical value for both customers and the bakery.
