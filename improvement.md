# Additional Improvement

## Improvement Chosen

A dynamic "Today's Scheduled Bakes" availability board was added near the top of the page.

This feature automatically displays products scheduled to be available on the current day using JavaScript.

---

## Why This Improvement Was Added

During the diagnosis stage, one of the biggest problems identified was that customers could not quickly determine which products were available on a particular day.

Customers had to read through the full weekly menu and work out availability for themselves.

Marcus also explained that many customers access the website while commuting or travelling and need information quickly. This made product availability an ideal area for improvement.

---

## How It Works

JavaScript checks the current day and compares it with the bakery's weekly baking schedule.

Products scheduled for that day are automatically displayed in the availability board. The current day is also shown to help users understand why particular products are listed.

The feature displays products based on the bakery's schedule rather than real-time stock levels.

---

## Value for Customers

This improvement provides two main benefits for customers:

- Customers can immediately see which products are scheduled to be available without reading the entire weekly menu.
- Mobile users can make faster decisions about whether to visit the bakery while travelling or commuting.

---

## Value for the Business

This improvement supports Marcus's business goals by:

- Increasing the visibility of products available on a particular day.
- Encouraging more walk-in visits by helping customers quickly identify products they are interested in.
- Making the website a more useful tool for promoting daily bakery offerings.

---

## Reflection

This improvement directly addresses one of the most important issues identified during the diagnosis stage. While the feature does not provide real-time stock information, it still offers a significant improvement over the original design by making scheduled product availability far easier to access and understand.

Customers can find relevant information more quickly, while the bakery benefits from increased visibility of its daily products and services.
