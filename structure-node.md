# Structure Note

The page structure was designed around the needs of The Corner Loaf's customers, particularly commuters who often check the website on their phones while walking or travelling to work.

The hero section appears first because it quickly introduces the bakery and communicates its key message: fresh bread and pastries baked daily. This helps visitors immediately understand what the bakery offers.

The "Available Today" section is placed directly beneath the hero section because the owner identified that customers often struggle to determine whether products are available. By prioritising this information, visitors can quickly decide whether it is worth stopping at the bakery.

The pre-order and call bakery actions are positioned immediately after availability information. This reduces effort for customers who want to reserve products or contact the bakery while on the move.

The weekly menu appears next because it provides more detailed information for customers who want to browse the bakery's regular offerings.

The About section helps communicate the bakery's warm, local identity and reinforces the owner's goal of making the website feel more personal and authentic.

The FAQ section answers common customer questions without distracting from the primary goal of helping visitors find products quickly.

The contact section appears near the bottom because customers may need location, opening hours, and contact details before visiting the bakery.

A simple footer provides navigation links and reinforces the bakery's branding while keeping the overall design clean and uncluttered.
