# Additional Improvements

## Improvements Chosen

Two improvements were added to make the bakery website easier to use: product filtering for the weekly menu and a collapsible navigation menu for smaller screens.

Product filtering lets customers browse the weekly menu by category without reloading the page. The hamburger menu gives mobile users a compact way to open and close the site navigation.

## Why These Improvements Were Chosen

The diagnosis found that customers needed to find products and important pages more easily. The weekly menu contains many items, so category filters reduce the amount customers need to scan. On small screens, showing every navigation link in one row takes space and can make navigation difficult to use, so the collapsible menu gives those links a compact place.

Both improvements were achievable within the project scope and address different parts of the browsing experience.

## How the Features Work

### Product Filtering

Customers can select **All Products**, **Bread**, **Pastries**, or **Snacks**. The menu updates immediately: matching products remain visible, other products are hidden, and a message reports the results. Customers can return to the full menu by choosing **All Products**.

The filters make it easier to browse and compare products, especially for returning customers who already know which category they want.

### Mobile Navigation Menu

On smaller screens, a menu button opens and closes the navigation links. This keeps the header compact while still giving customers access to the site sections.

## Value for Customers

- Less menu content to scan when looking for a category.
- Easier comparison of products in the same category.
- Quick access to all products again.
- A more compact navigation layout on smaller screens.

## Value for the Business

- Makes more of the bakery’s product range easier to discover.
- Helps customers move between important site sections on mobile.
- Supports a simpler browsing experience for prospective customers.

## Trade-Offs and Limitations

The filters organise the menu but do not provide real-time stock information. Product and category data must be kept up to date when the menu changes. On mobile, customers need to open the menu before choosing a navigation link.

## Evaluation

Product filtering helps customers browse the weekly menu, while the collapsible navigation helps them move around the site on smaller screens. Together, they address the diagnosis findings about product discovery and mobile navigation without changing the bakery’s schedule or requiring a back-end service.
