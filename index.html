Pre-Order FAQ

Everything you need to know about placing a pre-order with us.

We ask for at least one day's notice before your chosen collection day. For large orders or custom items, two to three days is better. This gives us time to plan our bake and make sure everything is ready for you.

Come to the bakery at your agreed collection time. We will have your order bagged and labelled. If you are running late, let us know — we can usually hold orders for up to two hours after the agreed time.

Yes, but please let us know as early as possible. If we have already started baking your order, we may not be able to cancel it. Changes to quantity are usually fine if you give us a day's notice.

We do not currently offer delivery. All orders must be collected from the bakery in person. We are looking at delivery options for the future — follow us on Instagram to hear when that changes.

About The Corner Loaf

Marcus started The Corner Loaf after years of baking at home for friends and family. What started as a hobby turned into a proper small bakery when demand grew too much to keep up with. Everything is made by hand in small batches, no mass production, no shortcuts.
