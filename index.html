    Our story

    A Little About Our Bakery

    Marcus started The Corner Loaf after years of baking at home for friends and family. What began as a simple love for baking gradually grew into a small local bakery as more people discovered his handmade treats.

    Today, we continue to prepare our breads and pastries in small batches, with care, quality ingredients and a passion for bringing freshly baked goodness to the East Legon community.
